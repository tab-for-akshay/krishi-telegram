# Product: Last Telegram (Krishi Telegram)

## Vision
Resurrect India's telegram service (shut by BSNL on 15 July 2013) as an AI farm-advisory telegraph for low-bandwidth rural phones.

## Problem
Indian farmers in low-connectivity areas need quick, actionable crop-disease and farming advice. Long web pages don't work on basic smartphones; WhatsApp groups are noisy; calling KVK takes time.

## Solution
A farmer types (or pastes) a question in English, Hindi, or Marathi. The app responds like a 1960s telegram: ALL CAPS, max 20 words, "STOP" between sentences, priced per word like the original telegraph service. The reply is also encoded as playable Morse-code audio and printable on a Halloween-themed ghostly vintage telegram form.

## Target Users
- Small-hold farmers across Maharashtra and India
- Low-bandwidth rural mobile users (390 px screens)
- Literate in English, Hindi, or Marathi

## Key Experience Pillars
1. **Nostalgic authenticity** — sepia paper, flickering candle, ghost operator aesthetic
2. **Radical brevity** — 20-word cap forces crisp, actionable advice
3. **Safety first** — pesticide dose and self-harm questions are never forwarded to the model
4. **Offline-friendly UI** — single HTML page, minimal JS, no framework
